# Commercial direction / 商业方向

## Positioning / 定位

Reminder Intake / 聊完别忘 helps people who plan in AI chats carry the next step into their existing reminder tools with fewer decisions.

帮助习惯在 AI 对话里规划工作的人，把下一步带进已有提醒工具，减少重复填写和决策。

## What is free / 免费部分

The MIT instruction skill, installation guides, examples and community fixes remain open source. Existing MIT releases cannot retroactively become exclusive paid software.

MIT 技能、安装说明、示例和社区修复公开免费；已发布的 MIT 版本不能追溯变成独占收费软件。

## Paid hypotheses, not available products / 收费假设，尚未上线

1. Assisted setup: configure a supported host and the user's own authorized reminder tools.
   安装配置服务：协助连接用户自己的提醒工具。
2. Companion service: reliable cross-device follow-up, deduplication and user-controlled schedules.
   配套服务：跨设备跟进、去重及用户控制的时间安排。
3. Team workflows: shared task ownership and follow-up status with explicit access control.
   团队方案：负责人、跟进状态及明确的访问权限。

Do not promise these features until implemented and verified. Hosting, notification delivery, privacy, support costs and platform permissions must be assessed before charging for a service.

这些功能实现并核验前不对外承诺。收费服务前需评估托管、通知送达、隐私、支持成本及平台权限。

## First validation / 首轮验证

Recruit 10 voluntary testers. Measure installation completion, first verified reminder, repeated use after seven days, unwanted proposals and decision count. Ask whether users would pay for setup or ongoing delivery; record actual commitments separately from compliments. Do not invent adoption or conversion figures.

先招募 10 位自愿测试者，观察安装成功、首个核验提醒、七天后复用、打扰次数与决策次数。分别验证安装服务和持续送达的付费意愿，把实际承诺与口头好评区分。不得编造用户数或转化率。

Proceed to a paid pilot only when the core workflow reliably solves a recurring problem and support effort is understood. Pricing remains unvalidated; no income guarantee is made.

核心流程稳定解决重复问题、支持成本明确后，再开展付费试点。价格尚未验证，不承诺收入。
